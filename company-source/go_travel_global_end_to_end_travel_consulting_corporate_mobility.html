Who We Are

More Than a Booking Desk — Your Trusted Travel Partner.

Modern travel is rarely just a flight ticket. Behind every international client pitch, leadership offsite, or multi-country family holiday lies a maze of changing consular rules, tight layovers, hotel contracts, and last-minute schedule shifts.

At Go Travel Global (a group company of WorkHour Business), we treat every journey as a responsibility, not a transaction. Built on a strong foundation of corporate visa management and executive stay solutions, we bridge high-precision business mobility with thoughtfully curated leisure experiences across Europe, Asia, Africa, Nepal, and India.

Our Vision

To be the preferred global travel partner for businesses and leisure travelers—recognized for clarity, compliance, and seamless end-to-end execution.

Our Promise

Transparent pricing, vetted global ground partners, compliant documentation, and real human support from pre-departure briefing until safe return.

How We Deliver

From First Brief to Safe Return — Zero Friction.

Every corporate delegation and family vacation follows our structured 6-stage quality workflow.

Step 01

Requirement Capture & Profiling

We align on your business goals, traveler preferences, budget guardrails, and timeline before recommending routes.

Step 02

Visa & Route Feasibility Check

Immediate verification of embassy lead times, transit rules, and multi-city flight connections to eliminate bottlenecks.

Step 03

Custom Costing & DMC Selection

Transparent, itemized itinerary options backed by our vetted international hotel and ground-transport partners.

Step 04

Visa Filing & Confirmed Bookings

Hands-on application review, appointment scheduling, and locked-in air, hotel, and insurance confirmations.

Step 05

Pre-Departure Briefing Pack

Consolidated digital travel dossier with immigration tips, local SIM/forex guidance, weather advisories, and emergency contacts.

Step 06

24/7 On-Trip Ground Support

Active monitoring throughout your journey with rapid rebooking and local ground escalation whenever plans shift.
